# Agba V1 Role Model

Agba sits between the Oga/CEO and department heads or supervisors.

## Roles

### Oga / CEO
- Sends reports and business information.
- Gives Agba decisions, instructions, and context.
- Can confirm reports from any department.
- Sees the company-wide business picture.

### Department Head / Supervisor
- Sends reports for their department.
- Gives Agba operational information for their department.
- Can confirm reports belonging to their own department.
- Sees their department-level picture.
- Cannot confirm another department's reports.

### General Staff
- No direct reporting access in V1.
- No direct business-information access in V1.
- No report confirmation access.

## Evidence rule

Department reports are not trusted company facts merely because they were received. They are retained as pending evidence until confirmed by the submitting department head/supervisor or the Oga/CEO.

CEO-provided company information and decisions do not require another person's confirmation.

## Product principle

Agba is the operating brain between leadership and departmental operations. Department heads are the accountable reporting layer. The Oga has company-wide authority.
