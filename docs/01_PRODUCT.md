# 01 Product

## Product definition

Agba is an AI company operating brain. It turns departmental activity into governed company memory, identifies what matters, and communicates decisions, risks, priorities, and exceptions to the people who need them.

Agba is not a chatbot bolted onto a dashboard. The core loop is:

```text
Report → Normalize → Store → Understand → Connect → Reason → Brief → Act → Learn
```

## V1 users

### CEO

The CEO can access full company intelligence, including cross-department trends, financial signals, issues, decisions, goals, reports, and Agba's synthesis.

### Department Head

A Department Head can submit reports, ask Agba about their department, inspect department state, follow up on tasks and issues, and receive relevant company context that policy explicitly permits. Department Heads do not receive unrestricted access to other departments.

## V1 interface

### Conversation

The main interface. A user can report naturally, ask questions, challenge Agba, request summaries, and follow up on previous context.

### Agba's Office

The CEO's compact executive cockpit. It shows current company state, exceptions, priorities, decisions, financial signals, and what Agba believes requires attention. It should answer "what is happening?", "what needs me?", and "what changed?" quickly.

## V1 outcomes

Agba V1 must:

- know the company structure;
- know who owns each department;
- ingest daily reports in natural language;
- turn reports into structured facts and observations;
- maintain traceable evidence for important conclusions;
- track tasks, expenses, revenue records, issues, decisions, approvals, and goals;
- respect CEO versus Department Head access boundaries;
- produce a useful daily company briefing;
- answer grounded questions with citations to internal evidence;
- record important decisions and changes in company memory.

## Non-goals for V1

- autonomous financial transactions;
- autonomous employee discipline or HR decisions;
- unrestricted cross-department access;
- a large BI suite;
- replacing an accounting system;
- replacing departmental workflow software;
- pretending uncertain information is fact.
