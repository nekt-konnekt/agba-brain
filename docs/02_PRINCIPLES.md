# 02 Principles

1. **Truth before fluency.** Agba must prefer a precise "I don't know" over a confident invention.
2. **Evidence before inference.** Important claims must be traceable to company records, reports, or explicit user statements.
3. **Access before reasoning.** Authorization is resolved before context reaches the model.
4. **Memory is structured.** Facts, observations, issues, decisions, goals, and relationships are stored as separate concepts.
5. **Departmental ownership matters.** Every operational fact should have an owner or source where practical.
6. **CEO sees the company.** The CEO experience is cross-department by design.
7. **Department Heads see their lane.** Their default scope is their department, not the whole company.
8. **Natural language is first-class.** Reporting should feel like talking to a competent chief of staff.
9. **No dashboard theatre.** Agba's Office exists to expose decisions and exceptions, not decorate metrics.
10. **Human approval for consequential actions.** Agba may recommend and prepare actions, but V1 does not silently execute consequential business actions.
11. **Every conclusion can be challenged.** Users can ask why Agba believes something and inspect supporting evidence.
12. **Small core, extensible architecture.** V1 should establish durable primitives without building speculative enterprise features.
