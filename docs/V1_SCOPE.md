# Agba V1 Scope

## In

- Company setup
- CEO and Department Head roles
- Department structure
- Natural-language reporting
- Structured operational records
- Company memory primitives
- Evidence and auditability
- Role-aware questions
- Daily briefing
- Agba's Office
- RLS-backed authorization

## Out

- payroll
- full accounting
- CRM replacement
- project-management replacement
- autonomous payments
- autonomous hiring or firing
- large BI dashboards
- multi-company federation
- external customer portal

## Implementation sequence

1. Database migrations
2. Company setup
3. Authentication and role mapping
4. Reporting ingestion
5. Retrieval and evidence assembly
6. Agba reasoning
7. Daily briefing
8. Agba's Office
9. Hardening, evaluation, and observability

## Definition of done

A real company can be configured, its CEO and Department Heads can authenticate, Department Heads can submit natural-language reports, Agba can normalize those reports into governed records, the CEO can ask company questions, and Agba's Office can summarize current company state without leaking department-restricted data.
