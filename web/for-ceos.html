COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers CEOs actually need.

What is Agba?

Agba is executive operating intelligence for growing businesses. It connects department reports, company context, risks, decisions and follow-ups into one executive picture.

How does Agba work?

Agba follows Report, Understand, Connect, Remember, Alert, Decide and Follow up. Department heads report naturally and Agba connects the information into leadership context.

Does Agba replace department reporting?

No. Agba makes reporting more useful. Teams can report through channels they already use, including Telegram, while Agba structures and connects the information.

Who is Agba for?

Agba is designed for growing businesses with multiple departments, particularly companies in the roughly 5 to 100 employee range.

Can department heads report through Telegram?

Yes. Telegram can be used as a reporting channel so department heads can send updates naturally while Agba maintains shared company context.
