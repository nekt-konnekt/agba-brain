CEO QUESTIONS

Answers about Agba for CEOs.

Why would a CEO use Agba?

A CEO uses Agba to get a connected operating picture of the business, surface what matters, understand risks and track decisions and actions without manually reconstructing updates from different sources.

How is Agba different from a business dashboard?

A dashboard primarily displays metrics. Agba is designed to connect operational context, remember important business events, reason over related signals and communicate what deserves attention and what action may be needed.

Can Agba understand information from different departments?

Yes, when company policy permits it. Agba is designed to connect information across departments while respecting CEO and Department Head access boundaries.

Does Agba make decisions for the CEO?

Agba provides grounded analysis, priorities, risks and recommendations. It does not independently grant itself access or replace the CEO's authority over company decisions.
