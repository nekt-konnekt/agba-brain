ANSWERS

Questions people ask about Agba.

What is Agba?

Agba is an AI operating brain for businesses. It connects reports, orders, payments, issues, decisions and goals into company context, then helps users understand what matters and what to do next.

What does Agba do for a CEO?

Agba gives a CEO a connected operating picture of the business. It helps surface risks, priorities, changes, outstanding issues and important decisions without requiring the CEO to reconstruct the story manually.

Is Agba just a chatbot?

No. Agba is designed as an operating intelligence layer. Conversation is one interface to a system that can report, normalize, store, understand, connect, reason, brief, act and learn.

What information can Agba understand?

Agba is designed to work with business reports and operational records including revenue, expenses, orders, customers, suppliers, tasks, issues, decisions, approvals and goals, subject to configured access rules.

Does Agba remember previous business context?

Yes. Agba maintains governed company memory so important reports, decisions, unresolved issues and changes can provide context for later questions and decisions.

Who is Agba for?

Agba is designed for CEOs and department heads who need a reliable operating picture of the business. Access to company information is governed by role and policy.
