AGBA VS DASHBOARDS

A dashboard shows information. Agba helps leadership understand what it means.

Agba is executive operating intelligence for growing businesses. It connects department reports, company context, risks, decisions and follow-ups rather than asking leadership to inspect another collection of charts.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Is Agba a business intelligence dashboard?

No. Agba is designed around operating context and executive attention. Business intelligence dashboards are useful for inspecting metrics and analytical data. Agba focuses on connecting the human and operational signals around the business and surfacing what deserves attention.

THE DIFFERENCE

Traditional dashboard

  • Shows selected metrics and charts.
  • Usually requires someone to open and interpret it.
  • Often answers “what is the number?”
  • Can leave operational context across emails, chats and departments.

Agba

  • Connects reports from across departments.
  • Maintains useful company context over time.
  • Looks for relationships, exceptions and risks.
  • Helps answer “what changed, why does it matter and what should we do?”
THEY CAN COEXIST

Agba is not trying to eliminate good reporting.

Business intelligence tools can remain valuable for financial analysis, performance reporting and detailed metrics. Agba sits at a different layer: connecting those signals with departmental updates, decisions, commitments and unresolved issues so leadership can see the operating picture.

What is the difference between Agba and a dashboard?

A dashboard is generally a place to inspect information. Agba connects information across the operating business and surfaces relationships, exceptions and follow-ups that may require attention.

Does Agba replace business intelligence software?

Not necessarily. BI and Agba can be complementary: BI provides analytical reporting and metrics, while Agba focuses on broader operating context.

Less hunting for information. More understanding of the business.
